2026-10-29-02-automatic-visual-history-baseline

Automatic visual history baseline

Established the automatic archive workflow. No website design change; this baseline preserves all three toggle states on desktop and mobile for future comparisons.

Change notes

2026-10-29-03-radial-daylight-as-the-only-view

Radial daylight as the only view

Removed the classic uniform ring and Day lengths switch. Radial daylight is now the only visualization, with a left-aligned 12-hour reference switch. Brought the solstice labels closer to the contour and balanced the optical spacing of all four seasonal labels.

Change notes

2026-10-29-04-mineral-blue-limestone-and-ochre-palette

Mineral blue limestone and ochre palette

Palette-only refinement: replaced the grey-green midpoint with warm limestone at 12 hours, softened winter into mineral blue and summer into ochre gold, and interpolated in OKLab. Kept the existing shared daylight scale, geometry, calculations, layout and controls unchanged.

Change notes

2026-10-29-05-restore-the-brighter-sunny-gold

Restore the brighter sunny gold

Restored the original sunny gold endpoint (RGB 214, 166, 57), keeping the refined mineral blue, warm limestone midpoint, perceptual interpolation and all other behavior unchanged.

Change notes

2026-10-29-06-tighter-equinox-color-transitions

Tighter equinox color transitions

Concentrated the warm limestone transition into a narrower range around twelve hours using a smooth symmetric contrast curve. Blue and sunny gold now occupy more of the year away from the equator, while the exact equatorial midpoint, color endpoints, daylight data, radial geometry, layout and controls remain unchanged.

Change notes

2026-10-29-07-continuous-solstice-blue-to-yellow

Continuous solstice blue to yellow

Explored a new color direction: one continuous perceptual gradient from deep winter blue to bright summer yellow, following solstice phase throughout the year and reversing in the Southern Hemisphere. Removed the limestone midpoint and tightened transition curve. Radial lengths still show actual daylight hours; interface and layout are unchanged.

Change notes

2026-10-29-08-all-yellow-daylight-palette

All-yellow daylight palette

Explored a monochromatic yellow direction from deep golden saffron on shorter days to luminous sun yellow on longer days. Color again follows actual daylight duration on a shared continuous scale, preserving an almost uniform equatorial ring. Kept radial geometry, calculations, labels, layout and controls unchanged.

Change notes

2026-10-29-09-day-and-night-balanced-through-paper

Day and night balanced through paper

Explored a diverging day/night palette: blue strengthens below twelve daylight hours, yellow strengthens above twelve, and both independently meet at a quiet warm paper midpoint. The transition is soft and narrow, with no direct blue-yellow blend. Equatorial locations remain pale and nearly uniform; radial geometry, astronomical data and controls are unchanged.

Change notes

2026-10-29-10-four-celestial-turning-point-inflections

Four celestial turning-point inflections

Added precise, restrained color inflections on the actual astronomical event dates: deeper winter blue, luminous summer yellow, and paper-light equinox peaks. Each event has a soft approach across neighboring days with a distinct center. Seasonal photoperiod range controls emphasis so the equator stays quiet. Radial daylight measurements, geometry and interface remain unchanged.

Change notes

2026-10-29-11-restore-the-unaccented-day-night-palette

Restore the unaccented day-night palette

Rolled back the four turning-point color inflections, restoring the preceding blue-to-paper-to-yellow daylight palette. Removed event-specific color emphasis while preserving the experiment in the archive. Geometry, data, labels and controls are unchanged.

2026-10-29-12-restore-the-original-blue-to-gold-palette

Restore the original blue-to-gold palette

Restored the exact original six-stop blue-to-gold palette and RGB interpolation from before all color experiments, including the muted grey-green midpoint and original sunny gold. Retained the current radial-only view, balanced seasonal labels, reference-line control, astronomical data and every previous history snapshot.
